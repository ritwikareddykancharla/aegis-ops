Abstract

Unconstrained tool-calling agents frequently propose service restarts before checking deployment diffs or execute mutating commands without operator sign-off. AegisOps enforces an explicit read-only Model Context Protocol (MCP) investigation DAG across CloudWatch/Prometheus metrics, OpenTelemetry distributed traces, CodeDeploy/PLC change history, and SRE runbooks, synthesizes a written Root-Cause Analysis via Amazon Bedrock (us.amazon.nova-lite-v1:0), scores trajectory quality with a calibrated SRE rubric (95.8% human agreement), and blocks 100% of unapproved rollbacks.

02Problem & AWS team alignment

During a production incident, jumping directly from an alarm to a service restart hides the true causal trigger and risks compounding data loss. Furthermore, evaluating incident agents solely on their final summary misses broken intermediate tool calls, hallucinated parameters, and verbosity bias in naive LLM-as-a-Judge prompts.

AegisOps directly targets AWS DevOps Agent, Agentic AI DevOps, and Eva Data Center Automation: walking telemetry, distributed traces, software deployments, and physical data-center PLC cooling loops (Pod B14 Chiller Actuator Drift) over schema-validated MCP tools while calibrating its postmortem grader against 24 human SRE-scored incidents.

Position & Verbosity Calibration

Raw rubric prompts prefer longer postmortems and flip winners under candidate (A, B) vs. (B, A) swaps. Length-normalized scoring calibrated on 24 human SRE anchors achieves 95.8% agreement (κ = 0.917) and 100.0% prompt-order swap consistency.
